A few improvements to how you explore and trust the data on Scholar Data.

## Metrics dashboard

A new [Metrics](/metrics) page shows platform-wide statistics, including datasets by year, top contributing institutions, and breakdowns of datasets by **publisher** and **license**.

## Faster dataset pages

We sped up how dataset pages load, so metrics and related information now appear more quickly.

## Status page

A new [Status](/status) page shows live response times for Scholar Data's services, so you can check system health at a glance.
