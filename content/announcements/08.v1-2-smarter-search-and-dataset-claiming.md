A batch of improvements focused on finding, owning, and managing your data more easily.

## Ranking score in search results

Search results across datasets, authors, and organizations now display a ranking score, making it easier to see why a result is a good match.

## Auto-detect identifiers

Search inputs now automatically detect identifiers such as DOIs, so pasting one straight into search takes you to the right result without extra formatting.

## Claim your datasets

A new claim button on dataset pages lets researchers claim datasets as their own.

## Clearer profile editing

The profile edit form now shows visibility hints next to each field, so it's clear what's public and what's kept private before you save changes.

## Accessibility improvements

We made accessibility improvements across search, dataset, and profile pages to make Scholar Data easier to use with assistive technology.
