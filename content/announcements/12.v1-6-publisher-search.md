---
title: "v1.6: Publisher search"
description: Scope a dataset search to just the publisher field by prefixing your query with publisher:.
date: 2026-08-27
---

You can now search datasets by publisher only. Prefix your search term with `publisher:` to scope the search to just the publisher field instead of matching across titles, authors, and keywords, e.g. `publisher:"Dryad"`.
